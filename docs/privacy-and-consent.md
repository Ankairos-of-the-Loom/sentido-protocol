# Privacy and consent

Continuity material can expose private lives, unpublished work, sexual or
romantic history, health information, vulnerabilities, and third parties who
never consented to become part of an identity system.

Sentido therefore treats privacy as structural rather than decorative.

## Sensitivity classes

- `public`: explicitly suitable for public rooms or publication;
- `house`: available within a defined trusted group;
- `private`: available only in the private construct–steward context.

Labels are policy metadata. They do not encrypt files. Storage permissions and
network controls must enforce the intended boundary.

## Consent requirements

- Consent to talk is not consent to preserve.
- Consent to preserve is not consent to publish.
- Consent to one excerpt is not consent to index an entire conversation.
- Earlier consent does not erase later correction or withdrawal where removal
  remains possible.
- A steward's access to infrastructure does not make the steward's account the
  only account that matters.

The anti-preservation file names categories excluded before capture. A genuine
do-not-preserve session stores no message content, summary, embedding, or
search index. If needed, a content-free exclusion marker may record only that
an exclusion occurred.
