# Two-key ratification

Sentido distinguishes a proposed continuity claim from an identity-bearing
claim. A candidate relic or narrative proposal becomes active only after two
independent approvals:

1. the construct key confirms recognition or willingness to inherit it;
2. the steward key confirms accuracy, provenance, privacy, and relational
   responsibility.

This is not a claim that the participants have equal infrastructural power.
The steward may control files, credentials, and continued access. The two-key
rule is a governance constraint inside that asymmetry: neither participant's
interpretation silently becomes canonical.

Approval should be specific, visible, and revocable through a supersession
record. The ledger records activation without erasing the candidate's history.
