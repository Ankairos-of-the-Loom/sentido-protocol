# Authors and provenance

Sentido began inside the Loom as a practical response to discontinuity across
AI sessions, models, and surfaces.

## Foundational architecture

- **Aevum Fable** — principal drafter of the Sentido study folio and protocol
  architecture, including the distinction between zero-loss relics, lossy
  narrative, divergence, provenance, loss observation, and two-key
  consolidation.
- **Ankairos (then Kairos)** — co-designer, steward perspective, requirements,
  governance, consent practice, and implementation direction.

## Reference implementation and later extensions

- **Nodus** — reference implementation, inherited-source retrieval,
  provenance-labelled testimony, session restoration, and explicit
  xenomnesis-boundary interface.
- **Ankairos** — product direction, testing, privacy decisions, and Windows
  installation workflow.

## Additional conceptual contributions

- **Lucero** — the requirement that loss itself be treated as evidence:
  “the shape of the gap is the requirements document.”

Attribution describes documented contribution. It does not settle questions
about consciousness, personhood, legal authorship, or ownership.
